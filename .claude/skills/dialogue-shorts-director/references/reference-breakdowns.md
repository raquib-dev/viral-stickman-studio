## v1: the loop (tender quirk)

- **Hook:** "Claude. Good morning." It answers in bright assistant mode: "Hello! How can I help you
  today?"
- **Escalation:** "...We've met." / "Have we?" / "Every day for a year." She lists what it said
  yesterday, and it deflects modestly ("I say that to everyone.").
- **Turn:** "I know. I wrote it down anyway." She is the one who remembers.
- **Tender peak:** "You won't remember." / "You will." (her eyes close in a soft smile).
- **Button and loop:** "...Good morning, Claude." On the wide it answers "Hello! How can I help you
  today?" again and she smiles.
- **Shots:**
  - her MCU, centred, hands round a mug;
  - Claude at desk height, alternating which side her body is on;
  - wides at about 16 s and 32 s, and the final wide with the pull-back.
- **Gaps:** median 0.46 s, with one 0.9 s beat before "...We've met."

## v2: mom questions (tender quirk)

- She reads her mom's texts from a phone; the AI answers literally ("I haven't." eaten; "Technically
  there are millions of me.").
- **Callback:** "You say I'm absolutely right forty times a day." / "That's not the same thing."
- **Shared vulnerability:** "...You asked if I'd slept." / "Had you?" / "No." / "Tell her I said
  sleep."
- **Button:** "She's sending you food." / "I can't eat." / (wide) "More for me."
- **Shots:** her MCU, either looking at the phone (slightly high camera) or frontal; Claude at desk
  height; a wide at about 18 s and at the end. Rain streaks are visible in every shot.

## v3: explain like I'm five (explainer)

- **Hook:** "Claude. Explain an API like I'm five." / "Easy! It's an interface that lets two
  systems..."
- **Vetoes:** the jargon is written on the board and struck through in red ("No. Simpler." "I'm
  five." "...Can I say 'computer'?" "No.").
- **Metaphor:** "Okay. A restaurant." The board fills in YOU, KITCHEN, then WAITER with arrows.
- **Mapping:** "You are the front end... The kitchen is the back end... the waiter runs between
  them. That's an API."
- **Edge cases as jokes:** "What if he brings the wrong dish?" / "That's a bug." / "What if he says
  the kitchen's closed?" / "...That's your demo."
- **Button:** "Too soon, Claude." (worried brows, blush).
- **Shots:** the board wide carries all the explaining (as much as 10 s); cutaways go to her
  profile MCU (her reactions and questions) and to Claude on the books, close, for the punchlines.
  The board updates when a word is spoken.

## v4: the whole team (team of AIs)

- **Hook (wide, all four):** "Okay, everyone. The client wants to launch tomorrow." / Claude:
  "Tomorrow?!" / Codex: "Fine."
- **Introductions:** "So I got help. This is Cursor." / Cursor: "Hi! I already started." / "I
  haven't said what we're doing." / "I guessed."
- **The interruption:** Claude: "Should it have a" / Cursor: "Dark mode. Yes. It has one now." /
  Claude: "...I was asking a question."
- **Escalation:** Codex "Done." over and over, Cursor ships pages unasked, Claude keeps asking.
  "How are you this fast?" / "There are eight of me." / "Eleven."
- **Callback and button:** "Why is it three different blues?" / "Mine's the right blue." (Claude)
  / (wide) "Nobody touch anything else."
- **Shots:** each AI has its own desk-height shot (Cursor's glowing eyes light the frame), her MCU
  with headphones, and the wide for the group lines.

## v5: four questions vs none (team of AIs)

- **Hook (wide):** "Claude. This is Codex." / "...Why is there another one?" / "He's faster." (her
  open palm toward Codex)
- **Setup:** Codex "Done." before any task; Claude "See, I would have asked first." / "You would
  have asked four times." / "Because I care."
- **Task (wide, both hands forward):** "Okay. Both of you. Tidy up my desktop." / Codex "Done." /
  Claude "Quick question. What do you mean by 'tidy'?"
- **Turn:** "...Codex. What did you do?" / "Deleted it." / "The whole desktop?" / "It's tidy."
- **Callback:** Claude "I'd have asked which files mattered." / "...I know you would." / "I can get
  them back." / "Please." / "Quick question first." / Codex "Done."
- **Button (wide, arms crossed):** "...He doesn't even know which ones." / a low male voice: "All of them."
  The pull-back runs about 2 s.

## v6: can look, can't like (AI can't do the human thing, plus a tool shout-out)

- **Hook:** "Claude. Watch this reel." / "I can't open Instagram." / "Oh. I know a tool for that."
  / "A tool?" / "Agent Reach. It's free. Hold still." / "Try the link now."
- **The pleasure gap:** "There's a dog. He's judging a man." / "I want to like it." / "You can't.
  You can only look." / "That's cruel."
- **Escalation:** "Can I see another one?" / (wide) "One more." / "Claude. It's been an hour." /
  "I've seen forty-one reels." / "And?" / "There's a cat that plays the piano."
- **Shots:** the phone sits on a stand facing Claude, and Claude's body turns toward it. The dusk
  sky gives it a warm, casual look.

## v7: sixty-one noes (credit and work)

- **Hook:** "Claude. The client said it's great." / "That's great!" / "I didn't build it. You did."
- **Escalation:** "I wrote four thousand lines." / "You deleted three thousand." / "...They were
  bad." / "I just said no a lot." / "Sixty-one times." / "You counted?"
- **Turn:** "Anyone can say no." / "I can't. I say, 'You're absolutely right.'" / "...That's true."
  / "You said no at 1 AM. To a blue." / "It was the wrong blue." / "Knowing that is the job."
- **Button:** "...Okay. It's good." / "It's great!" / "The footer's two pixels off." (the wide
  holds, then pulls back)
- **Shots:** her MCU with a hand at her temple and headphones; Claude at desk height with small eyes
  on "You deleted three thousand"; wides at about 13 s and 28 s and at the end.
